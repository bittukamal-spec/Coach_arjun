# Arjun logo — prompts for AI image tools

Part of `docs/PREMIUM-PLAN.md`. Goal: a **flat** logo mark of Teer's head that stays clear from a 16 px browser tab to a 1024 px app-store icon. Keep the 3D Teer for illustrations.

**Best tools for logos:** Ideogram (best with clean shapes and text), Recraft (can export real SVG vector files — ideal), ChatGPT image, Midjourney.

**Tips**
- Upload one of the 3D Teer images as a reference and say "same character, as a flat logo".
- Make 4–8 versions, pick 2, then say "simpler" once more. Logos get better when simplified.
- Check every pick at tiny size: zoom out until it is the size of a fingernail. If you can still see the face, it works.
- Don't put text inside the icon itself. "Arjun" is added next to it in code.

## 1. App icon / logo mark (main one)

```
Flat vector logo mark of a cute round mascot head for a sports mental-coaching
app called "Arjun". A perfect circle head in royal blue (#1B4DFF), a sun-yellow
(#FFC629) sweatband across the forehead with a small white arrow pointing right
on it, a small golden feather with a white tip tucked into the band at the top
right, two big friendly eyes with white highlights, a small calm smile.
Flat design, 2–3 colours only, bold simple shapes, no gradients, no 3D, no
shadows, no text, centered, generous padding, plain white background,
looks clear at very small sizes, modern app icon style, vector, minimal.
```
Midjourney add: `--ar 1:1 --style raw --v 6.1 --no text, gradient, shadow, 3d`

## 3. Wordmark (if you also want a custom "Arjun" lettering)

```
Wordmark logo reading "Arjun" in a friendly, bold, rounded geometric sans-serif,
royal blue (#1B4DFF), lowercase "rjun" with a capital "A". The crossbar of the
"A" is a small sun-yellow (#FFC629) arrow pointing right. Flat vector, no
```

Note: AI tools often misspell words — check every letter. Ideogram is the most reliable for text.

## Rules (same as the mascot brief)

- Original. Must not look like any other brand's logo or mascot (for example Athlete Mindset's "Aomi").
- Colours: blue `#1B4DFF`, sun `#FFC629`, dark ink `#101533`, white. Small touches of coral `#FF6B4A` are fine.
- Files to send: **SVG** if the tool offers it (Recraft does), otherwise PNG at least **1024 × 1024** on a plain background.
- You need full commercial rights (paid plan / tool's terms).

## What Claude does with it

Turns the chosen design into a clean SVG in code, then updates every place at once: app icon (all sizes), browser tab icon, website header/footer, sign-in, Coach header, Founder Dashboard, emails.
