# Arjun mascot — prompts for AI image tools

Part of `docs/PREMIUM-PLAN.md`, Step 4. Use these with **Midjourney**, **ChatGPT (image)**, **Ideogram** or **Leonardo**. Make the **main character first**, pick the one you like, then make the poses using that same character as the reference image (Midjourney: `--cref <image link>`; ChatGPT: upload it and say "same character").

**Rules for every image**
- Must be **original**. Do not ask for, or accept, anything that looks like Athlete Mindset's "Aomi" (the brain character) or any brand mascot.
- Transparent or plain white background (so it can be cut out cleanly).
- At least 2000 × 2000 px. Download the highest size.
- Check the tool's terms: you need commercial use rights (Midjourney paid plan; ChatGPT images are fine for commercial use).

---

## Concept 1 — "Arjun", the young athlete coach

### Main character
```
Original 3D mascot character for a sports mental-coaching app called "Arjun".
A friendly young Indian athlete, about 16, round soft face, short black hair,
calm confident smile, big kind eyes. Wears a royal blue (#1B4DFF) sports
headband with a small white arrow symbol, a royal blue track jacket with sun
yellow (#FFC629) trim, carries a small stylised wooden bow on his back.
Soft clay-like 3D style, smooth rounded shapes, gentle studio lighting, soft
shadows, premium modern app mascot, full body, standing, facing camera,
plain white background, centered, high detail, 4k.
```
Midjourney add: `--ar 1:1 --style raw --v 6.1`

### Poses (use the main character as reference)
1. **Waving hello** — `same character, waving hello with one hand, warm smile`
2. **Listening / thinking** — `same character, hand on chin, listening carefully, thoughtful expression`
3. **Breathing calmly** — `same character, eyes closed, shoulders relaxed, slow calm breath, peaceful`
4. **Pointing** — `same character, pointing to the side with one hand, encouraging expression`
5. **Celebrating** — `same character, both arms up celebrating, big happy smile, small confetti`
6. **Coach with notes** — `same character, holding a small clipboard, friendly coach look`
7. **Head only (for small icons)** — `same character, head and shoulders only, front facing, smiling`
